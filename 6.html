Beware, the doctors lie to you at this clinic. I recently went down for 3 weeks of treatment for breast cancer. Even though I told them I didn't want their chemo treatment, they assured me it would be really helpful, they would only be using 10% and I would not lose my hair. They lied. I lost all of my hair which started 1 week after I returned home. When contacting my doctor he told me that was so unusual and recommended Minoxidal (which actually makes your hair shed worse before it regrows). This happened to 2 other women I kept in contact with. So it wasn't that unusual. When I followed up to find out what they gave me, it was the MOST toxic of all the chemotherapy treatments and they gave me a 50% dose, not 10%. It makes me question everything they are administering here. It has been extremely stressful which is opposite from what I need healing cancer. I am guessing they push chemo so much because they want results. I later found out the type of cancer I have, isn't responsive to chemotherapy. I would have hoped a clinic like this would have tests to check this. The total disrespect for me to make an informed decision makes me motivated to share my story to help protect others. I regret going here. Spend your money with doctors who are honest.

I will say that the nurses were spectacular. They deserve a 10 star rating. But the doctors deserve a -10 for taking advantage of their patients at their most vulnerable.
